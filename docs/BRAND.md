# OCHRÉ brand rules

Read this before changing the look, the words, or a name. The private operating document governs money and phasing. This file governs what the public site may show.

## Palette

Sampled from the brand sheet, 21 November 2026. Gold is the flat reading of the metallic swatch, not the specular highlight.

| Name | Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight | `--navy` | `#051632` | Ground, footer, primary button |
| Midnight 2 | `--navy-2` | `#071836` | A second navy, when a panel needs a step |
| Gold | `--gold` | `#C6A85A` | The Cherub, one rule, one italic word |
| Antique gold | `--antique` | `#A87F35` | The one emphasis on a porcelain ground |
| Porcelain | `--porcelain` | `#F4F3EF` | Page ground |
| Bone | `--bone` | `#E0CDA8` | A warm panel, the prototype band |

Gold is a line or a word. It is not a fill, a gradient, or a glow. Square corners. No glass, no neon.

`--gold-ink` (`#6E4F1C`) is antique gold darkened so small labels stay readable on porcelain.

## Type

Open-licence, self-hosted with `next/font`, Latin and Latin Extended (for É).

| Role | Face | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark and labels | Cinzel | `OCHRÉ`, eyebrows, buttons |
| Headlines | Cormorant Garamond | Titles. One italic word. |
| Body | EB Garamond | Paragraphs |
| Numbers | Azeret Mono | Edition counts, the editor’s meter |

Six roles, not six sizes on one screen. Do not set body text in bold. Do not put two kinds of emphasis on one line.

## The Cherub

The public name of the mark is **the Cherub**.

Never use **Cherubim** as a mark, a line, or a product name.

The mark is a gold winged head above the `OCHRÉ` wordmark. The SVG in `src/components/brand/cherub.tsx` is a line redraw from the brand sheet. The sheet file was too small to trace the face, so this is not a master outline. It needs a designer pass before it is cut, engraved, or embroidered.

Use it as the lockup (hero, header, footer). Do not tile it behind products.

## The lattice

The reduced seraph, whorl, and small diamond from the sheet appear once: the footer band (`LatticeBand`). Not as wallpaper, and not behind a product.

The Cherub, and any sacred text, stay off the floor and off anything worn below the waist. A headband may carry the wordmark. Shorts, socks, and rugs do not carry the Cherub.

## Voice

Quiet, exact, ceremonial. One house, four lines: Art, Home, Garms, Jewellery. The promise is one sentence: bring an image or an idea, and OCHRÉ makes one physical object to order, numbered, with a certificate and a provenance passport.

Say **House of Junaid**. Never **Royal**.

A call to action commissions a piece. It does not offer print-on-demand.

## Names that stay on /about

The Cherubim and Seraphim church, its founders, and Holy Michael are written on `/about` only. They do not appear on a product, a line, a label, or a passport.

## What is not for sale

No celebrity likeness and no third-party mark on saleable work (decision D6). Fashion and music references in the private notes are internal. Do not draw them onto a piece.

Do not invent a price or an edition size. Do not publish a Gaeilge, Yoruba, Hebrew, or Arabic line on an object until a native speaker has checked it.

Fela the Goat is an existing house image already in the collection. Treat any new likeness the same way as D6: it does not go on sale until the rights are cleared.

## Concept renders

If a picture is a render of something that has not been made, label it **Concept render**. The salon headband render from the concept preview is not on the site. The page says the object has not been made.
