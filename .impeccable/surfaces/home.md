# Home

Mode: experience

The homepage is the first room of the house. A visitor should see one real work large, read a wall label, and understand that Ochré makes numbered objects to order. The four lines are a contents page. Editions follow as plates. Commissioning (`/make`) and the collection are both available, and neither is a shimmering control.

Composition reference: a headline beside a full plate, then a row of works, in the manner of Isle Thorne Collective. Do not import its type, colour, or code.
