# Artwork

Mode: experience

`/collection/[slug]` is the wall the work hangs on. The plate is the largest thing on the page. The label lists title, year, medium, dimensions, and edition, and it updates when the visitor changes material or size. Inspect opens a zoomable lightbox with that caption. Collect and Add to basket stay on the page, outside the dialog. Edition count, price, and the certificate note stay factual and use the existing catalogue and order count.
